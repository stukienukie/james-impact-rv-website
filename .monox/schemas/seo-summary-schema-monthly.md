---

RULES FOR CLAUDE CODE WHEN WRITING THIS SUMMARY:
- Never fabricate numbers — only write what the screenshots actually show
- If a value cannot be found in the screenshots write: Not available
- All narrative and team note language must be specific to this client and this month — never generic
- Vary the narrative intro and team note every month — the client reads this every month and it must feel fresh
- The team note must sound human — warm and encouraging, like it came from someone who actually worked on their account
- Every website change listed must include both what was changed AND why it helps in plain English
- Never use SEO jargon in the narrative intro, team note, key win, or next month focus
- ARP going down = improvement — make sure movement direction reflects this correctly
